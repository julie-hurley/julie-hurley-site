My approach

Strategy First. Always.
Here’s My Approach

I build content systems around what your audience needs, what your business needs to accomplish, and what the data tells us to do next.

Great strategy isn’t about producing more content; it’s about knowing which content earns its place. I treat SEO, AEO, business goals, and audience research as the foundation everything else gets built on.

My process

Six Phases, From Research to Results

With 10+ years of combined experience leading B2B and B2C content strategy from ideation through performance tracking, I specialize in blending SEO and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) with AI-powered workflows, having built successful content systems for 160+ enterprise and small-business accounts across multiple industries. Below is my approach.

Select a phase to see the details

01

Discovery & Research

I always audit existing multi-platform content and research the audience before moving forward. This includes:

  • Audience Analysis: Demographics, core needs, pain points, and trends.
  • Data & Search: Quantitative reviews, search intent mapping, and user behavior.
  • Competitive Landscape: Business goals, branding research, and competitor gap analysis.
02

Find the Opportunity

I identify the gaps and priorities to determine what should be created, updated, consolidated, or retired.

  • We start with the business problem, audience needs, and search behavior, not a predetermined format.
  • The right solution might be new content, or it might be restructuring, updating, or building a content network. I have the expertise to know the difference.
03

Build the Strategy

I map out intentional pillar-and-cluster frameworks, editorial direction, and customer journey alignment.

  • Build unique, high-engagement content plans with clear optimization recommendations and measurable success criteria.
  • Resonate with the audience on every platform while aligning with business objectives.
04

Create, Execute & Implement

This is where strategy meets content governance. I write, edit, direct, collaborate, design, create visuals, and publish while keeping the work aligned with the strategy and your brand.

  • UX & Search Optimization: Built for UX, SEO, and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO).
  • Structural Clarity: Enhanced reading and scannability so both users and search engines can easily navigate and act.
05

Measure the Results

I use tools like GA4, Search Console, Semrush, and Ahrefs to monitor organic visibility and see exactly what changed.

  • Tracking Metrics: Visibility, rankings, traffic, engagement, product sales, and progress toward the business goal.
06

Improve & Refine

Audience and search trends are always shifting. Strategies always need to be revisited over time.

  • Learn from the Data and Integrate: I feed performance data back into the strategy to continuously refine and optimize your marketing strategy and assets.

In my mind, there is no finish line: Search and audience preferences are always changing, and content should change with them. I never stop at publication.
